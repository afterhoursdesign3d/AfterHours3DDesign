About

After Hours Design began as an idea to bring the attitude of late-night creativity to furniture and industry, where form and intention come together and fit seamlessly into the home.

Through rounds of development, individual designs and brand concepts came about, some of them born from ideas that originated elsewhere.

The brand will keep developing over the years, and its core principles will always be a prominent part of future projects and expansions.
