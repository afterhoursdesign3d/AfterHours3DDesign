Pricing

01Quotes

All quotes are determined by the scale, complexity, timescale and deliverables required for the individual project. This is based on an hourly estimate multiplied by my current rate.

02Deposit

A deposit of half the full quote is required to secure my time and begin work. The other half must be paid before the final deliverables are released.

03Revisions

As standard you are entitled to two revisions. Any revisions after that are billed hourly and added to the final invoice. Each stage has a set review window, delays sending revision feedback will extend the final delivery schedule.

04Delivery

All deliverables will be released on schedule in a Google Drive folder.
